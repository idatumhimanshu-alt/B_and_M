Project Overview & Aesthetic Intent
Project Type: A high-end, conversation-first landing page for a prestigious Landscape Architecture firm.

User Goal: The firm deliberately removed their traditional portfolio to focus entirely on human connection, trust, and dialogue. The primary conversion point is an enquiry form and direct contact methods.

Aesthetic/Mood: "Bold Sophistication." This must feel architectural and mature, but edgy. It should use significant negative space, strong grid lines, and high-impact typography. Think "brutalist-lite meets luxury minimalism."

Design System Parameters
Color Palette (Strictly Enforced):

Background: Off-White (Hex: #F9F7F4) or a very light Muted Olive Grey (Hex: #E8E5E2).

Primary Text: Deep Charcoal (Hex: #2C2A28).

Secondary/Accent Text: Muted Forest Green (Hex: #4B5D52) or Rich Earth Tone Brown (Hex: #705F52).

Action/CTA: The main call-to-action button must be the accent color (#4B5D52 or #705F52) with a subtle hover state to a darker shade.

Typography:

Headings (H1/H2): A dramatic, ultra-bold, contemporary Grotesk font (e.g., Neue Haas Unica Bold or Monument Extended). Extremely large leading (line height) for a sculptural look.

Body Text: An elegant, highly readable modern serif (e.g., Garamond Premier or Lora) for the manifesto text to convey expertise and storytelling.

Layout & Grid: 12-column desktop grid. Heavy use of asymmetrical layouts and generous white space—elements should feel "placed" rather than crammed.

Interactive Elements:

Buttons: Pill-shaped or sharp-edged rectangular solid buttons.

Form Fields: Clean, minimal, high-stroke-weight underline fields only, rather than boxed inputs. On-focus interaction should trigger a subtle underline color change.

Section-by-Section UI Breakdown
1. Hero Section (The Hook)

Layout: Left-aligned text occupying 80% of the vertical viewport height. The right side is negative space.

Content Hierarchy:

Headline (Massive Typography): "Skip the Scroll."

Sub-headline (Large Body Font): "Let’s Talk."

Intro Manifesto (Body Font, single column): "In a world drowning in digital noise, we chose a different path. We believe great work isn't discovered by passive browsing—it’s forged through dialogue, shared vision, and radical trust."

UI Details: A single, bold arrow icon pointing down near the fold to indicate scrolling.

2. Manifesto Section (The Philosophy)

Layout: A strong two-column layout. Left column has a bold H2 title. Right column has the body text.

Content:

Title (H2): "Relationships over Algorithms."

Body Text (Serif): "We aren't here to impress a search engine or fill a grid with endless case studies. We are here to connect, collaborate, and create impact. Our best work lives in the spaces we’ve shaped and the relationships we’ve kept."

UI Details: Heavy negative space. A thin vertical line separating the columns to enforce the architectural feel.

3. How We Work Section (The Method)

Layout: A three-card grid layout.

Content:

Card 1 Title: Conversations over Catalogs

Card 1 Text: Every project starts with a real, unfiltered human conversation about what you are trying to build.

Card 2 Title: Reputation over Reach

Card 2 Text: We rely on trust and referrals, not paid impressions or flashy landing pages.

Card 3 Title: Intent over Noise

Card 3 Text: No corporate jargon, no fluff—just raw focus, bold execution, and absolute transparency.

UI Details: Each card should be a clean, thin-bordered container. A subtle, clean line icon above each title (e.g., a microphone, a handshake, a target).

4. Enquiry Form (The Conversion)

Layout: Full-width section with a dominant form.

Content:

Title (H2, above form): "Tell Us Who You Are."

Instruction Text: You aren't looking for a cookie-cutter vendor. You’re looking for a partner. Drop your details below and tell us a bit about what you're chasing.

Form Fields (Minimalist underline):

Your Name / Organization (Required)

Email Address (Required)

What are you building or looking to solve? (Text area/multiline)

Submit Button (Bold, Accent Color): "Submit & Start the Conversation"

UI Details: The entire section background should be slightly darker than the main body (e.g., #E8E5E2) to differentiate the form area.

Content:

Title (H2): Skip the form. Connect Directly.

WhatsApp (Right Column):

Text: Scan to chat instantly with our core team.
